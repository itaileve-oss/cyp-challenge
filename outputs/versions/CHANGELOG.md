## 2026-09-29 — `cyp_activity_submission_2026-09-29.csv`
**Recalibration only — no model/feature changes from 2026-09-24.**

The 2026-09-25 real leaderboard scores let us solve each isoform's true blind
Pearson correlation directly (`rho_true = (R2_blind + k^2) / (2k)`, k = our
local OOF Pearson at that submission), instead of assuming local rho ≈ true
blind rho as the old calibration did. Applied as a per-isoform inflation
factor (`rho_true_today = INFLATION[iso] * rho_local_today`) in
`build_test_submission.py`:

| Isoform | Inflation (rho_true / rho_local) | rho_local (today) | rho_true (used) |
|---|---|---|---|
| CYP1A2 | 1.27x | 0.553 | 0.703 |
| CYP2C9 | 1.19x | 0.657 | 0.782 |
| CYP2D6 | 1.49x | 0.423 | 0.630 |
| CYP3A4 | 0.96x | 0.788 | 0.756 |

CYP1A2/CYP2C9/CYP2D6 local CV was *pessimistic* relative to the true blind
population (inflation > 1) — their prediction spread had been under-scaled.
CYP3A4 local CV was *optimistic* (inflation < 1) — plausible explanation for
CYP3A4's regression on the 2026-09-24 submission, where the old calibration
(k=rho_local, no correction) over-stretched its spread. See
`check_cyp3a4_rho.py` analysis: with the old calibration, CYP3A4's observed
blind R²=0.574 was already within 0.001 of its theoretical ceiling
(rho_true^2=0.575) — meaning the *scale math* wasn't the bug, the *input rho*
was slightly overconfident. Today's rho_true-based scale (x0.756 instead of
x0.788) corrects for that directly.

  donor was the key addition beyond the literature's 3-point description).
  Blended in at weight 0.50. Biggest single-isoform gain of the session.
- **CYP2C9**: unchanged. 16 distinct approaches tried and confirmed null
  (docking under 2 aggregation strategies, acidic-group presence/distance,
  the generalized azole/pyridine/sulfonamide coordinating-group definition,
  Arg108-anchored distance, the CYP1A2 pharmacophore template, logD, strain
  energy, 3D shape descriptors, L1 loss, confidence-interval-weighted
  training). CYP2C9 has the widest confidence intervals relative to its own
  label spread of all 4 isoforms (~90% CI-width/std ratio vs 54-80% for the
  others) — likely a genuine data-noise ceiling, not a missing-feature gap.
